Puppy Quest privacy policy

Last updated: [DATE]

Puppy Quest is a game made by [YOUR NAME] and Emily. We take children's privacy seriously, so the game is built to collect nothing.

  • No personal information is collected. The game does not ask for names, emails, photos, location or contacts, and has no accounts or sign-in.
