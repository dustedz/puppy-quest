Puppy Quest privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Puppy Quest is a game made by Jack and his daughter Emily. It is made for kids, so it is built to collect nothing at all.

  • No personal information is collected. The game does not ask for names, emails, photos, location or contacts, and has no accounts or sign-in. Names you give your plush puppies stay on your device.
  • No internet use. The game runs entirely on your device and does not send data anywhere.
  • No ads, no tracking, no in-app purchases, no chat. The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or other SDKs.
  • Saved games stay on your device. Progress is stored only on the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.
  • Photo mode pictures stay on your device. When you tap Save in photo mode, the picture of the game is saved to your device's Photos. It is never uploaded or shared unless you choose to share it yourself.
  • No outside links. The game does not open web pages or other apps.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is released.

Questions? Contact us through the Puppy Quest project page.
